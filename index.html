You have the night shift at the Lantern Hollow Puppet Theater. It closed years ago. The puppets never got the message. Survive until 6 AM.

SURVEILLANCE SYSTEM // BUILDING POWER UNSTABLE // STAFF: 1

  • Open the cameras with Space and track Pip, Hob, Mirelle, the Crowkeeper and Stitchface across all 11 cameras.
  • Hob only moves when you are not looking at him.
  • Hear footsteps or see eyes in a doorway? Shut that door. Flash the light to check.
  • Doors, lights and the monitor all drain the power. Mirelle is drawn to the light.
  • If the power dies, something with a lantern comes to find you.
  • There are 20 nights and you play them in order. Every night is harder than the one before.
